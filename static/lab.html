3–6 Story conversation

The full loop: the narrator reads a beat and asks you something, you answer out loud, StoryBuddy transcribes and scores it, and the AI companion reacts before moving on. Finished stories are saved to sessions.json.

Your turn

7–8 Progress report

Every finished story is saved to sessions.json. The report uses spoken sessions only; sessions with typed answers are kept but left out, because they have no voice measurements. Demo sessions are made-up practice history, clearly labelled, so there's a trend to report on.
